Support

You’re here. This page is for product questions, not crisis care.

How to reach us

For product questions or technical problems, you can currently contact the project through its public support page. Please do not include phone numbers, health information, contact details, or other private information.

Report a technical issue on GitHub

Back does not use accounts, so there is no login to recover. Please do not include a phone number, a “my person” contact, or other private details in a report. Those stay on your device and we cannot see them.

If you need urgent help

Back is not an emergency service. If you think you may need urgent medical help, contact your local emergency service. In the app, Extra support can show a public emergency number for a country you choose, and it can open your phone app. Back does not place the call for you.

Privacy

What you save in Back stays on this device. Read the Privacy Policy.

Back is quiet support for the next minute. It is not a medical device and is not therapy.
