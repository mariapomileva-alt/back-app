Support

You’re here. This page is for product questions, not crisis care.

How to reach us

Back does not use accounts, so there is no login to recover. For questions about the app, open a GitHub issue on the public repository:

github.com/mariapomileva-alt/back-app/issues

Please do not include a phone number, a “my person” contact, or other private details in an issue. Those stay on your device and we cannot see them.

If you need urgent help

Back is not an emergency service. If you think you may need urgent medical help, contact your local emergency service. In the app, Extra support can show a public emergency number for a country you choose, and it can open your phone app. Back does not place the call for you.

Privacy

What you save in Back stays on this device. Read the Privacy Policy.
