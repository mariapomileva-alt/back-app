Back Plus

Keep Back close

Back is built for the next minute — six quiet tools on your device, with no account required. Back Plus is an optional way to support the app and unlock more ways to make Back yours when it is ready. The core tools stay free.

What stays free

  • All six tools: Breathe, Distract, Ground, Move, Listen, and Read
  • Extra support and public emergency numbers you choose
  • Call my person and your on-device support contact
  • Privacy-first design — your patterns and contacts stay on this device

Back does not lock safety tools or sessions behind a paywall. Nothing in Back replaces crisis care or therapy.

What Back Plus includes

When Back Plus launches, it is meant to add more sounds, exercises, and personalization — not to take away what already helps you today.

  • Expanded environment sounds for Listen
  • Additional appearance themes
  • More Read library content
  • Deeper My patterns insights

These items are in development. Only features marked as available in the app or on this page at launch will be part of Back Plus.
