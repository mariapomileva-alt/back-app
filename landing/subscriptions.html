Subscriptions

Back is free to use. There is no subscription required today and no in-app purchase in this version.

Current version (1.0)

All six tools in Back work without payment. The App Store listing for this version does not include an active subscription or one-time purchase product. You do not need an account to use Back, and nothing in the app is locked behind a paywall.

If a paid tier is added later

If we introduce an optional paid tier (for example “Back+”), this page will describe what it includes, the price, how renewal works, and how to cancel through your Apple ID subscription settings. Purchases would be handled by Apple; Back would still not require a Back account for the core app.

Until then, there is nothing to buy inside Back and no recurring charge from Back itself.

Questions

For product or billing questions, use Support. For how we handle data, see the Privacy Policy.
