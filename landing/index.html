Back does not require an account. Personal activity and patterns stay on your device. Core tools and bundled sounds work without an internet connection.

  • No advertising tracking

    Questions

    Common questions

    Is Back free?
    Yes. Today every tool in Back is free to use. A paid plan called Back Plus is planned for later and is not available yet.
    Does it work without internet?
    Yes. The six tools work offline. Audio and exercises are bundled on the phone.
    Do I need an account?
    No. Back does not ask you to create an account.
    What data does Back collect?
    The iOS app does not collect your data on a server. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Preferences and a support contact you save stay on this device.
    Is Back therapy or a medical app?
    No. Back is a self-help tool. It is not therapy, a diagnosis, a medical device, or an emergency service.
    Is Back on Android?
    Not yet. Back is available for iPhone on the App Store.
