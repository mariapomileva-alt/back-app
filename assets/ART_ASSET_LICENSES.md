## Release checklist

Before store submission:

1. Confirm that every selected file complies with the image-generation service terms applicable to the account and generation date.
2. Complete a visual similarity review and reject any asset that is materially similar to a known third-party illustration or brand asset.
3. Confirm that no unintended watermark, signature, logo, text, or recognizable protected character is present.
4. Confirm that all edges and transparency render correctly on Warm Earth, Soft Sage, and Forest/Deep Green themes.
5. Record any human edits, derivative versions, animator, contractor, or external source below.
6. Keep source masters and final optimized app exports under version control.
