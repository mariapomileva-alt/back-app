Distinct-session honesty
The English pack is large enough for 50 distinct sessions. A session is a
pattern + thread + least-recent items from each slot. With 9 patterns, 9
threads, and hundreds of local items (stories, curious, words, observe,
notice), repeating the same opening sequence inside 50 visits is unlikely
once memory and cooldown are working. This is combinatorial variety, not
50 hand-authored scripts.

Do not claim infinite or generated content. Do not fetch more text at runtime.
