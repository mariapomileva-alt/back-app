# Back Ground — premium illustration set

Seven illustration states matching the default Feet & Body grounding sequence:

1. Feel where your feet meet the floor.
2. Gently move your toes.
3. Notice the weight of your feet.
4. Feel what is supporting your body.
5. Notice your hands.
6. Let your shoulders soften.
7. Stay here for one more moment.

Use `premium-stills/` when Reduce Motion is enabled. Use the corresponding transparent animated WebP from `premium-loops/` otherwise. Render with `contentFit="contain"`; do not crop feet, hands, shoulders, or supporting surfaces. Illustrations are decorative and should be hidden from screen readers because the synchronized instruction contains the meaning.

## Additional Ground modes

- `mode-5-4-3-2-1` — sensory attention gathering around a stable center.
- `mode-look-around` — a wide, open field designed to encourage visual scanning.
- `mode-texture-temperature` — a hand exploring cool, warm and soft materials.

Each mode has a static PNG and a matching six-second animated WebP. These are mode-level hero scenes; internal prompts remain text-led.
