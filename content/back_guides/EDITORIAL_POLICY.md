# Back Guides editorial policy

## Purpose

Back Guides offers calm, practical reading for people who are having a difficult moment or are preparing for one. It explains choices a reader can make; it does not diagnose, treat, or promise to stop panic.

## Non-negotiable safety rules

- Back is a self-help tool, not therapy, a medical device, emergency care, or a substitute for professional care.
- Never tell a reader that chest pain, breathlessness, dizziness, or any other physical symptom is "just panic." New, severe, worsening, or worrying symptoms need medical assessment.
- Never promise that a technique will stop a panic attack, work in a stated number of minutes, or work for everyone.
- Never advise a reader to ignore an emergency, avoid prescribed care, stop medication, or delay professional help.
- Every article includes a clearly visible urgent-help panel and links to Back's support page.
- Every article uses calm, non-judgmental language: "might," "if it feels okay," "you can switch," and "one small action."
- Do not use alarming photos, depictions of distress, medical imagery, or before/after recovery claims.
- Write as one caring person speaking to another, never as a clinical instruction sheet. Prefer short, ordinary sentences; acknowledge that the reader may be frightened or tired; offer choices rather than commands; and never manufacture cheerfulness.
- Guides visuals use a warm, tactile natural language: cream plaster or paper, muted moss and olive greens, soft window light, botanical shadows, stone, linen, or velvet-like texture. Images must remain calm and original, with no text, app UI, faces in distress, or medical symbolism.

## Product facts allowed in Guides

- Back has six local tools: Breathe, Distract, Ground, Move, Listen, and Read.
- Back does not require an account. The app's saved preferences and support-contact details stay on the device.
- Extra Support and Call my person remain available without a Back Plus subscription.
- A call or message is opened in the phone's system app and starts only after the person confirms.

## Editorial review

Automated checks cover structure, links, metadata, required safety copy, and prohibited claims. They do not replace editorial judgment.

- Owner review: product, brand, and publication authority.
- Product-editor review: lived-experience tone, app accuracy, and reader clarity.
- A publication is blocked until both approvals and the source hash are recorded.
- A publication is also blocked when local `main` contains unpushed commits, so a Guide release can never accidentally carry unrelated App Store or Google Play work.
